## Rating Scales & Guidelines

### Intelligibility (1–5)
- **5 (Completely Intelligible)**: Every word and phoneme is crystal clear without cognitive effort.
- **4 (Largely Intelligible)**: Clear overall; 1–2 minor blurred or muffled phones, but all words easily understood.
- **3 (Moderately Intelligible)**: Understandable with focused attention; some words or syllables ambiguous.
- **2 (Poorly Intelligible)**: Significant phonetic distortion or dropped syllables; less than 50% understood.
- **1 (Unintelligible)**: Completely degraded audio, unintelligible noise, or total failure.

### Overall Quality / Naturalness (1–5)
- **5 (Natural / Human-like)**: Indistinguishable from natural human speech; smooth prosody, realistic breathing and pauses.
- **4 (Good Quality)**: High naturalness with minor synthetic characteristics (slight robotic timbre or flat intonation).
- **3 (Fair Quality)**: Clearly synthetic/robotic, noticeable robotic pitch or energy jumps, but acceptable.
- **2 (Poor Quality)**: Strong metallic phasing, severe buzzing, clipping, or unnatural rhythm.
- **1 (Very Poor)**: Extremely robotic, harsh distortion, completely unnatural.
