## 3. Transparency & Discrepancy Disclosures

1. **Provisional Checkpoint Evaluation**:
   - The quantitative validation metrics (`val_metrics.json` and `val_metrics.md`) are grounded in checkpoint step 1000 evaluated on a CPU validation subset.
   - They reflect the exact mathematics and pipeline interfaces of `tools/eval_val.py`.
   - In accordance with the prompt guidelines, these metrics are prominently labeled as **Provisional Dry-Run Metrics** in both markdown tables and `inference_manifest.json` (`"provisional": true`).
2. **Text Discrepancy in Telugu Test Sentences**:
   - As documented in `AUDIT.md` (BUG-15), subtle text representation variations between the PDF assignment prompt and `test_sentences.json` were identified (T2b compound spacing, T3 space typo, T4 ending word).
   - Both representations are supported by the frontend; `test_sentences.json` preserves the exact test strings without mutation.
3. **Hardware Assessment**:
   - Local environment has no usable CUDA GPU (`check_env.py`: `CUDA available: False`).
   - All benchmarks and inference tests were validated in CPU mode with deterministic seeding (`torch.device("cpu")`), yielding an RTF of 0.28–0.34 on x86-64 CPU cores.
